A little about this place

Made for the love
of playing games.

Pixel Portal is a student-made browser game site built from curiosity, creativity, and a lot of tinkering. It is a place to find something fun, revisit a classic, and explore games without having to install anything.

Why Pixel Portal exists

This started as a way to learn more about web development and game design. Over time, it grew into a little arcade filled with browser games, collections, and experiments. The hope is simple: make it easy to find something enjoyable and give people a reason to come back.

What to expect

  • No Pixel Portal account is needed.
  • Games open right in your browser when their host allows it.
  • Favorites and recently played games stay in your browser on this device.
  • Some games are hosted by other sites, so availability can change.

A thank-you to the creators

Pixel Portal is possible because of the artists, developers, and platforms whose work makes browser gaming great.

  • Elthen's Pixel Art Shop — logo sprites.
  • honeyconebunny on Tenor — galaxy background artwork.
